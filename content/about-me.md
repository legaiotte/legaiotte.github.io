👋 Hello, I’m Leti
👀 I’m a scientist-turned-techie who somehow ended up making a career out of turning messy processes into smarter, simpler solutions. 😄

🌱 Currently, I work with Microsoft Power Platform, helping organisations automate processes, improve efficiency, and get more value from their solutions, especially with Power Apps, Power Automate, and the wider Power Platform ecosystem.

🧠 My background is a little unusual. I started programming way back in secondary school, then took a detour into Biology, where I completed both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees. Turns out, the scientist mindset: curiosity, problem-solving, asking “but why?” way too many times is actually pretty useful in tech.

I’m naturally curious, always learning, and probably asking too many questions. 🚀

💞️ This space is where I share what I’m building, learning, breaking, fixing, and discovering along the way.
I’m here to keep learning, share what I discover, connect with awesome people, and hopefully make someone else’s tech journey a little easier along the way.

So, if you’re into Power Platform, data, automation, problem-solving, or just figuring things out together, welcome! 💜

Grab a coffee ☕ and have a look around.
